FAQ: SPEED & HEAT

1. What is "Thermal Throttling"? ▼
When your processor gets too hot (>45°C), it intentionally slows down to cool off. This causes lag in games after 10 minutes of play.
2. Why is my score lower than my friend's? ▼
Benchmarks are affected by background apps, battery level, and heat. If your phone was hot *before* starting, your score will be lower.
3. What is "FLOPS"? ▼
Floating Point Operations Per Second. It measures raw math speed. Modern phones can do trillions of calculations per second.
4. Does higher RAM speed matter? ▼
Yes. Faster RAM allows apps to open instantly. Capacity (8GB vs 12GB) lets you keep *more* apps open, but speed determines responsiveness.
5. What is "Geekbench" vs this test? ▼
Geekbench is a native app test. This is a "Web Benchmark" running inside your browser. It tests how well your phone handles websites and web apps.
6. Does "Game Mode" help? ▼
Yes. It tells the OS to prioritize the game (or this benchmark) over background emails/notifications, boosting performance.
7. Why did my phone get warm? ▼
We pushed all 8 cores to 100% usage. Energy = Heat. A warm phone means the heat dissipation system is working correctly!
8. What is Single-Core vs Multi-Core? ▼
Single-Core handles simple taps (opening Instagram). Multi-Core handles heavy tasks (Video Editing, Gaming).
9. Can I overclock my phone? ▼
Generally no, unless you "Root" it. Manufacturers lock the speed to ensure the battery lasts all day.
10. Is 120Hz related to speed? ▼
120Hz is the screen refresh rate (smoothness). It requires a fast GPU to maintain, but they are separate metrics.
