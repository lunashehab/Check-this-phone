FAQ: USER EXPERIENCE

1. Why is my reflex time over 300ms? ▼
This is normal human reaction time! Pro gamers train to get ~180ms. If it's over 500ms, your phone might have high "Input Latency."
2. What is "Input Latency"? ▼
It's the delay between your finger touching the glass and the software registering the click. Gaming phones aim for <20ms.
3. Why does scrolling feel "choppy"? ▼
This is called "Jank." It happens when the processor can't render the list fast enough. 60 FPS is the minimum for smooth motion.
4. What is "Ghost Touch"? ▼
If the Aim Trainer registers a "Miss" when you didn't touch the screen, your digitizer might be damaged or dirty.
5. Does a screen protector affect this? ▼
Yes. Thick glass protectors can reduce touch sensitivity, causing missed taps in the Aim Trainer.
6. What is 120Hz vs 60Hz? ▼
Hz is how many times the screen updates per second. 120Hz feels 2x smoother than 60Hz but uses more battery.
7. Why use "TouchStart" vs "Click"? ▼
Standard "Click" events wait 300ms to see if you are double-tapping. "TouchStart" is instant, which we use here for accuracy.
8. Can I improve my score? ▼
Yes! Turn on "Game Mode" or "High Sensitivity" in your phone settings to prioritize touch response.
9. Why did I miss the target? ▼
It could be user error (fat finger) or a "Dead Zone" in the screen where touch isn't registered.
10. Is 0ms latency possible? ▼
No. Physics dictates some delay for the signal to travel from the glass to the CPU, but modern phones get very close!
