FAQ: HARDWARE SPECS

1. Why does my GPU say "Adreno"? ▼
Adreno is the graphics chip used in Qualcomm Snapdragon processors (Samsung S-series, Pixel, etc.). It means you have a high-end Android device.
2. Why is my storage lower than advertised? ▼
A "128GB" phone loses about 20GB to the Operating System (Android/iOS) and pre-installed apps. You usually only get ~100GB of real space.
3. What is a "Logical Core"? ▼
Modern CPUs have multiple processing units (Cores). 8 Cores is standard for smartphones. It allows multitasking without slowing down.
4. Why does RAM say "~8GB" (Approx)? ▼
Browsers round down memory for privacy security (to prevent fingerprinting). If you have 12GB, it might still report as 8GB.
5. What is "WebXR"? ▼
It is the technology that detects VR Headsets (like Quest) or AR glasses connected to your phone.
6. Why is my GPU "Masked"? ▼
Some privacy browsers hide the exact model name of your graphics card to prevent websites from tracking your specific device ID.
7. Does this test drain battery? ▼
Slightly. Querying the GPU requires waking up the graphics processor, but the impact is minimal (less than playing a game).
8. What is "Mali" GPU? ▼
Mali GPUs are found in MediaTek or Exynos chips. They are common in mid-range phones or non-US Samsung models.
9. Can I upgrade my RAM? ▼
No. On smartphones, RAM is soldered directly to the motherboard and cannot be upgraded like a PC.
10. Why is the Gamepad dot gray? ▼
You must press a button on your controller for the browser to detect it. Connect Bluetooth, then press 'A' or 'X'.
