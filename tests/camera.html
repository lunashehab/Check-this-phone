FAQ: CAMERA & OPTICS

1. Why is the quality lower than my Camera app? ▼
Browsers (Chrome/Safari) throttle video quality to save battery and data. They rarely let websites access the full 48MP or 200MP raw sensor data.
2. What is "OIS"? ▼
Optical Image Stabilization. The lens physically floats on magnets to counteract your hand shaking. It's crucial for night photos.
3. What is an "Aperture" (f/1.8)? ▼
The size of the hole letting light in. A lower number (f/1.8) means a bigger hole = better night photos.
4. Digital Zoom vs. Optical Zoom? ▼
Optical Zoom uses physical glass lenses (better quality). Digital Zoom just crops the image (pixelated quality).
5. Why do I have 3 cameras? ▼
Usually: 1 Main (Wide), 1 Ultra-Wide (0.5x), and 1 Telephoto (3x/5x Zoom). The phone switches between them automatically.
6. Is "Megapixels" everything? ▼
No! A 12MP camera with a large sensor is often better than a 1080MP camera with a tiny sensor. Sensor size determines light intake.
7. Can websites spy on me? ▼
No. Browsers have a hard "Permission Wall." You must click "Allow" every time a site wants to see your camera.
8. What is "LiDAR"? ▼
Light Detection and Ranging. It shoots lasers to measure depth. It helps with fast autofocus in the dark and AR apps.
9. Why is selfie video mirrored? ▼
Humans are used to seeing themselves in a mirror. Non-mirrored video looks "wrong" to our brains. We mirror the preview here for comfort.
10. What is "ISP"? ▼
Image Signal Processor. It's a chip that processes raw data from the sensor into a JPG photo instantly (HDR, noise reduction).
