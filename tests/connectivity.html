FAQ: WIRELESS & 5G

1. What is "Ping"? ▼
Ping is the time (in ms) it takes for a signal to go from your phone to the server and back. Lower is better. <50ms is ideal for gaming.
2. Why is my 5G slow? ▼
5G signals are very easily blocked by walls and trees. If you have 1 bar of 5G, it is often slower than full bars of 4G LTE.
3. What is "Bandwidth"? ▼
Bandwidth is the *width* of the pipe. Speed is how fast water flows. High bandwidth means you can stream 4K video without buffering.
4. Is Public WiFi safe? ▼
No. Hackers can "sniff" traffic on public WiFi. Always use a VPN or HTTPS (Secure) websites when at a coffee shop.
5. Why does Bluetooth drain battery? ▼
It constantly scans for nearby devices. However, modern Bluetooth 5.0 (Low Energy) uses almost zero power when idle.
6. What is "Airplane Mode"? ▼
It shuts off all radios (Cellular, WiFi, Bluetooth) to prevent interference with airplane instruments. It is also the fastest way to charge your phone.
7. What is "Packet Loss"? ▼
When data gets lost in the air. 1% packet loss causes "rubber banding" in games or stuttering in video calls.
8. What is a "Hotspot"? ▼
Your phone turns its Cellular Data (4G/5G) into a WiFi signal that other devices can connect to. This drains battery quickly.
9. Why is my Location wrong? ▼
IP Geolocation is not GPS. It only knows where your ISP's main hub is located, which might be in a neighboring city.
10. What is WiFi 6? ▼
The newest WiFi standard. It handles "crowded" networks better, so your internet doesn't slow down when everyone is home.
