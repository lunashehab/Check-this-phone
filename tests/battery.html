2. THE 20-80 RULE To double your battery's lifespan, keep it between **20%** and **80%**. Charging to 100% creates heat and stress on the chemical ions.

FAQ: BATTERY & CHARGING

1. Is "Fast Charging" bad? ▼
Only because of heat. Fast charging generates massive heat, which degrades the battery chemistry. If your phone gets hot, unplug it.
2. Can I charge overnight? ▼
Yes. Modern phones have chips that cut off power at 100%. However, holding a battery at 100% for 8 hours is still slightly stressful for it.
3. What is a "Cycle"? ▼
One cycle is using 100% of the battery (e.g., draining from 100% to 0%, or 50% to 0% twice). Batteries usually last 500-800 cycles.
4. Why wireless charging is hot? ▼
Wireless charging is inefficient. About 30% of the energy is lost as waste heat. Heat kills batteries. Cable is always better.
5. Should I drain to 0%? ▼
NEVER. Lithium-Ion batteries hate being empty. Draining to 0% can cause the battery to become unstable or fail completely.
6. Why does 100% drop fast? ▼
Battery voltage isn't linear. The drop from 100% to 90% usually happens faster than 50% to 40% due to voltage curves.
7. When to replace the battery? ▼
When its "Maximum Capacity" (Health) drops below 80%. At this point, the phone may slow down (throttle) to prevent crashing.
8. What uses the most power? ▼
1. The Screen (Brightness). 2. GPS/Location. 3. Cellular Data (especially in low signal areas).
9. Does "Dark Mode" save battery? ▼
Yes! On OLED screens (iPhone/Samsung), black pixels are turned off completely, using zero power. It saves about 10-20%.
10. Are power banks safe? ▼
Yes, as long as they are from a reputable brand (Anker, Samsung, etc.). Cheap gas station power banks can have unstable voltage.
