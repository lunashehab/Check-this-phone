FAQ: BATTERY INTELLIGENCE

1. Is "Fast Charging" bad? ▼
Only due to heat. Fast charging generates heat, which degrades chemicals. If your phone gets hot, unplug it immediately.
2. Should I drain to 0%? ▼
NEVER. Lithium-Ion batteries become unstable at 0%. Aim to keep your battery between 20% and 80% for maximum lifespan.
3. What is a "Cycle"? ▼
A cycle is using 100% of the battery (e.g., 100% to 0%, or 50% to 0% twice). Batteries typically last 500-800 cycles before degrading.
4. Why does Wireless Charging get hot? ▼
It is inefficient. ~30% of energy is lost as waste heat during transmission. Wired charging is always cooler and healthier.
5. Does "Dark Mode" save power? ▼
Yes! On OLED screens, black pixels turn off completely. Dark Mode can save 15-20% battery daily.
6. How accurate is "Health Estimate"? ▼
It is an algorithmic guess based on voltage consistency. Only the operating system (iOS settings) can read the exact hardware chip data.
7. What is "Thermal Throttling"? ▼
When the battery gets too hot (>45°C), the phone slows down the CPU to prevent explosion risks. This causes lag in games.
8. Can I charge overnight? ▼
Yes, modern phones stop charging at 100%. However, holding it at 100% for 8 hours puts slight stress on the cells.
9. Why does 5G drain battery? ▼
5G requires more power to transmit signals, especially if the tower is far away. The modem has to "shout" louder to connect.
10. What is "Optimized Charging"? ▼
A feature that waits at 80% charge and finishes the last 20% right before you wake up, reducing time spent at stressful 100%.
