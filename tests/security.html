FAQ: SECURITY & PRIVACY

1. What does "Root Detected" mean? ▼
It means the safety locks on your OS are removed. Malware can easily steal data from other apps on rooted phones.
2. Is my FaceID/TouchID safe? ▼
Yes. We never see your biometrics. We only ask the phone "Is the user verified?" and the phone replies "Yes".
3. What is Hardware Encryption? ▼
A separate physical chip (Secure Enclave) that stores passwords. Even if hackers steal your files, they can't read this chip.
4. Why does it say "Restricted"? ▼
Browsers like Brave or Safari Private Mode block hardware scans to protect your privacy. This is actually a good sign!
5. What is SSL/HTTPS? ▼
It encrypts the "tunnel" between you and the website. Without it, anyone on your WiFi could read your passwords.
6. Can this page scan for viruses? ▼
No website can scan your phone's files for viruses. That requires an app download. We scan for *vulnerabilities*.
7. What is a "Jailbreak"? ▼
The iPhone version of Rooting. It removes Apple's security limits, making the phone vulnerable to attacks.
8. Why does my OS say "Linux"? ▼
Android is built on Linux! Seeing "Linux armv8" is completely normal and safe for Android phones.
9. Is "Incognito Mode" safe? ▼
No. It only hides history on *your* device. Your ISP and websites still see everything you do.
10. How do I fix a "Failed" score? ▼
Update your OS, remove any Root tools, and ensure you are browsing via HTTPS.
