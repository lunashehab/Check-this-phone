FAQ: NETWORKING

1. What is an IP Address? ▼
It's your digital home address. Public IP is what the world sees. Local IP is what your router assigns to your specific phone.
2. Why hide my IP? ▼
Hiding your IP (via VPN) prevents websites from tracking your location and stops hackers from targeting your specific connection.
3. What is a "WebRTC Leak"? ▼
WebRTC is used for video chat. Sometimes it bypasses VPNs and sends your real IP directly to the website. This is a privacy flaw.
4. What is "Latency" (Ping)? ▼
It's the time (in ms) it takes for data to travel from you to the server and back. Lower is better for gaming.
5. What is a "Port"? ▼
Think of ports as doors to your house. Port 80 is the front door (Web). Port 22 is the back door (SSH). You want unused doors locked (Stealth).
6. Why is my 5G speed slow? ▼
5G signal is easily blocked by walls. If you have 1 bar of 5G, it might be slower than full bars of 4G LTE.
7. What is "Jitter"? ▼
Jitter is the instability of your Ping. If Ping jumps from 20ms to 100ms rapidly, that is High Jitter (bad for calls/gaming).
8. What is "Packet Loss"? ▼
When data gets lost on the way. 1% packet loss causes stuttering in videos and lag in games.
9. How do I fix a WebRTC Leak? ▼
Use a high-quality VPN app that has "Leak Protection" or disable WebRTC in your browser settings (advanced).
10. Why use "Stealth" ports? ▼
If a hacker scans your IP and sees "Closed" ports, they know you exist. If they see "Stealth," it looks like an empty void.
