FAQ: SCREENS & DISPLAY

1. What is "Burn-In"? ▼
On OLED screens, displaying the same image (like a status bar) for hours can permanently "burn" a ghost image into the pixels.
2. 60Hz vs 120Hz? ▼
60Hz updates 60 times a second. 120Hz is twice as fast, making scrolling and animations look incredibly smooth.
3. What is a "Dead Pixel"? ▼
A pixel that receives no power and stays black. It cannot be fixed.
4. What is a "Stuck Pixel"? ▼
A pixel stuck displaying one color (usually Red, Green, or Blue). Sometimes, flashing colors rapidly can "unstuck" it.
5. Why does Black look Gray? ▼
You have an LCD screen (backlit). OLED screens (like iPhone Pro/Samsung S) turn off black pixels completely for "True Black."
6. What is "Ghost Touch"? ▼
When the screen registers a tap you didn't make. This is often caused by a cheap charger or a damaged digitizer.
7. Does brightness affect battery? ▼
Yes, massively. The screen is the #1 battery drain. Lowering brightness to 50% can double your battery life.
8. What is "PPI"? ▼
Pixels Per Inch. Higher PPI means sharper text. Anything above 400 PPI is considered "Retina" quality (invisible pixels).
9. What is "HDR"? ▼
High Dynamic Range. It allows the screen to get much brighter for highlights (sun/explosions) while keeping shadows dark.
10. Can I fix a cracked screen? ▼
No software can fix hardware damage. You must replace the glass digitizer assembly.
