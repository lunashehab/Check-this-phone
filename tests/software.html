FAQ: SECURITY & PRIVACY

1. What does "Root Detected" mean? ▼
It means the operating system's safety locks have been removed. This allows customization but lets malware steal data from other apps.
2. Is my FaceID/TouchID safe? ▼
Yes. Websites (like this one) NEVER see your fingerprint or face data. We only ask the phone "Is the user verified?" and the phone says "Yes/No".
3. What is Hardware Encryption? ▼
Modern phones have a separate chip (Secure Enclave) just for passwords. Even if a hacker steals your phone files, they cannot read this chip.
4. Why does it say "Restricted"? ▼
Some browsers (like Brave or Private Mode Safari) block websites from checking your hardware to protect your privacy. This is actually a good thing!
5. What is SSL/HTTPS? ▼
It means the tunnel between you and the server is locked. Without it (HTTP), anyone on your WiFi could read your passwords.
6. Can this test find viruses? ▼
No website can scan your phone for viruses. That requires app permissions. We scan for *vulnerabilities* (open doors), not the malware itself.
7. What is a "Jailbreak"? ▼
It's the iOS version of Rooting. It removes Apple's security restrictions. It is dangerous for banking apps.
8. Why does my OS say "Linux"? ▼
Android is built on top of Linux! Seeing "Linux armv8" is completely normal for Android phones.
9. Is "Incognito Mode" safe? ▼
It only hides history from your own device. Your ISP and the websites you visit can still see everything you do.
10. How do I fix a "Failed" score? ▼
Update your OS, remove any "Root" tools, and ensure you are not using a public, unencrypted WiFi network.
