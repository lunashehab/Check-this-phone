1. MOTOR TECHNOLOGY There are two types of vibration motors:
1. ERM (Old): A spinning weight. Slow to start/stop. Feels "Rumbly."
2. LRA (New): A magnetic mass on a spring. Instant response. Feels "Crisp." (e.g., Apple Taptic Engine).

FAQ: HAPTICS & VIBRATION

1. Why doesn't it work on iPhone? ▼
Apple blocks the `navigator.vibrate` API in Safari to prevent websites from creating annoying ads. It only works on Android.
2. What is "Phantom Vibration Syndrome"? ▼
A psychological phenomenon where you think your phone vibrated, but it didn't. Your brain misinterprets muscle spasms as notifications.
3. Does vibration drain battery? ▼
Very minimally. A vibration motor uses about 0.95% - 4% of battery over a full day. The screen uses 100x more power.
4. What is the "Taptic Engine"? ▼
Apple's brand name for a Linear Resonant Actuator (LRA). It can reach peak vibration in 10ms, vs 50ms for older motors.
5. Can strong vibration damage my phone? ▼
Generally no. However, extreme prolonged vibration *can* sometimes de-stabilize optical image stabilization (OIS) in cameras (common on motorcycles).
6. Why does my phone make a rattling noise? ▼
This indicates a loose component (like a camera lens or button) vibrating against the chassis, or a failing motor bearing.
7. What is "Haptic Feedback"? ▼
"Haptic" refers to touch. It's the tiny vibration you feel when typing or pressing a button to confirm the action was registered.
8. Why is the "Tick" pattern so weak? ▼
Short pulses (20-30ms) require a high-end LRA motor. On older phones, the motor can't spin up fast enough to be felt.
9. Can I increase vibration strength? ▼
Only in your system settings. Web apps cannot control intensity, only duration (Time On / Time Off).
10. What is an "SOS" pattern? ▼
It is Morse Code: 3 Short, 3 Long, 3 Short (... --- ...). This is the universal distress signal.
